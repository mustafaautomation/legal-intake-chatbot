## Flat fees

Some uncontested family matters and most immigration filings use a flat fee. Immigration flat fees are quoted at the consultation. The immigration screening consultation in this demo is $150 and is credited if the firm is retained. The chatbot will not invent a flat fee for a filing it has not been given a published number for.
