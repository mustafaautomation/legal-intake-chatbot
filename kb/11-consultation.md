## How a consultation works

A consultation is 30 minutes, by video or at 400 Harbor Street, Suite 1200, Ashford. The intake desk must finish a preliminary conflict screen before a time is confirmed. The meeting is a chance for a lawyer to decide whether the firm can help. It does not create an attorney-client relationship. That relationship starts only if both sides sign an engagement letter.
