## Safety and immediate handoff

If you are in immediate danger, call 911. For domestic violence in the United States, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788. Immigration detention can be looked up on the public ICE locator at https://locator.ice.gov. That lookup is not legal advice. A hearing tomorrow, detention, or a safety emergency stops the chatbot and opens a handoff to (555) 014-2200.
