## Hourly fees

Employment matters are usually hourly, in a published demo range of $350 to $475 an hour, sometimes with a hybrid. Family matters are usually hourly, in a published demo range of $325 to $450 an hour. A family retainer in this demo starts at $3,500 and is quoted at the consultation, not in chat. Screening consultations for personal injury, employment, and family are free and last 30 minutes.
