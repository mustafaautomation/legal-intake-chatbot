## Personal injury timeframes

General information only. This is not a deadline for your case, and it is not a conclusion that a claim is timely or late. People often ask how long they generally have to file a personal injury lawsuit. Many states use a period of about 2 years from the injury for a general personal injury lawsuit. Claims involving a government body are often shorter. Harlow & Pierce uses an 18-month window only as an internal intake filter. An attorney has to calculate the period for a real matter. The chatbot will not do that calculation.
