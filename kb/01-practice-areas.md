## Work the firm accepts

Harlow & Pierce LLP, a fictional firm in Ashford, accepts new inquiries in four areas: personal injury, employment, family, and immigration. Personal injury covers collisions, falls on a property, and other serious injuries caused by someone else. Employment covers firings, unpaid wages, harassment, and discrimination by an employer. Family covers divorce, custody, parenting time, and support. Immigration covers family petitions, removal defense, naturalization, and employment visas.
