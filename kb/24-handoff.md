## Talking to a person

You can ask to talk to a human at any time. Say “talk to a person” or use the button in the chat. The desk also opens a handoff when it cannot find a firm note with enough confidence, when a message sounds frustrated, or when the topic is a safety emergency, detention, or a hearing tomorrow. A person at the demo intake line is (555) 014-2200. The handoff is a ticket on the staff inbox, not a promise of immediate representation.
