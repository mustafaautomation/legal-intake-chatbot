## Work the firm does not take

The firm does not handle criminal defense, bankruptcy, patents, tax controversy, or real estate closings. Harlow & Pierce does not handle criminal defense. A question about those topics is answered from this note and is not opened as a consultation. The intake desk will say so plainly.
