## Contingency fees

Personal injury matters are typically handled on a contingency fee. The published demo figures are 33 percent of the recovery if the matter resolves before a lawsuit is filed, and 40 percent after filing. Case costs are separate from the fee. If there is no recovery, the client does not pay the attorney’s fee. These figures are examples for the fictional firm. The engagement letter, not this note, would control a real matter. The chatbot cannot estimate what a claim is worth.
