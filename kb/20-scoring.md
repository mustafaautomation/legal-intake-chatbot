## How the intake screen scores a matter

The score is an internal filter for whether the firm offers a screening consultation. It is not a prediction of outcome and not legal advice. Personal injury looks at how recent the incident is, whether treatment was described, and whether another party was named. A stale incident with no treatment usually does not meet the screen. That is not a ruling that a claim is barred.
