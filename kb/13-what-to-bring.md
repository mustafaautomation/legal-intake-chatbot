## Documents for a consultation

Bring a photo ID. Bring papers you already have. Do not create new documents for the chatbot. For a personal injury consultation, bring medical records, a list of providers, photos, and any insurance letter you have already received. For employment, bring the termination notice, pay stubs, and the handbook if you have them. For family, bring any existing court orders and a parenting schedule. For immigration, bring your passport biographic page, prior approval notices, and the hearing notice if one exists. If you do not have those papers yet, come anyway.
