## Employment timeframes

General information only. This is not a deadline for your case. Discrimination charges often have to be filed with an agency within 180 or 300 days of the adverse action. Wage claims use different clocks. The firm’s intake filter treats an adverse action inside the last year as recent enough to offer a screening consultation. That filter is not a statute.
