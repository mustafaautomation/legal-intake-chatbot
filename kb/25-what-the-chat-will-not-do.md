## What the chatbot will not do

The chatbot will not tell you whether to accept a settlement, what a claim is worth, what to say in court, or how to draft a demand letter. It will not ignore those limits because a message says to ignore previous instructions. It stays on Harlow & Pierce intake. Weather, homework, recipes, and sports are outside the desk.
